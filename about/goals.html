Who We Are Mission Vision History Goals
Core Purpose

Seven Long-Term Organizational Goals

NSTUANS envisions a university community empowered with knowledge, awareness, and positive values.

01

A Drug-Free Campus

Promote a safe, healthy, and drug-free environment within the university through active peer vigilance and protective initiatives.

02

Raising Awareness

Educate students about the physical, psychological, social, and economic consequences of narcotics and substance abuse.

03

Empowering Youth

Encourage students to become responsible leaders, peer counselors, and active advocates of anti-narcotics initiatives.

04

Promoting Healthy Lifestyles

Inspire young people to engage in constructive pursuits, physical sports, cultural enrichment, and academic innovation.

05

Strengthening Community Engagement

Extend awareness campaigns beyond the university perimeter, actively working with local schools, colleges, and social groups.

06

Encouraging Research & Innovation

Promote evidence-based understanding of addiction mechanisms, root causes, socioeconomic factors, and effective rehabilitation strategies.

07

Building Strategic Partnerships

Collaborate with educational institutions, healthcare professionals, government organizations, NGOs, and youth rehabilitation bodies.

Scientific Method

Seven Evidence-Based Research Goals

NSTUANS believes that effective drug prevention requires rigorous scientific knowledge, empirical data, and scholarly inquiry.

R-01

Understand Drug Abuse Patterns

Explore the prevalence, modern trends, and demographic patterns of substance use among young university students.

R-02

Identify Risk Factors

Investigate the social, economic, psychological, academic, and environmental pressures associated with student substance use.

R-03

Assess Social Impacts

Examine how addiction affects individual health, family relationships, academic retention, and campus social safety.

R-04

Evaluate Awareness & Attitudes

Measure and study students' knowledge, perceptions, stigmas, and attitudes toward narcotics through cross-faculty surveys.

R-05

Explore Prevention Strategies

Identify effective intervention models, peer counseling frameworks, and community-level rehabilitation approaches.

R-06

Promote Evidence-Based Solutions

Conduct empirical surveys, field studies, and research publications to guide university policy and regional stakeholders.

R-07

Encourage Academic Collaboration

Create structured opportunities for students, faculty supervisors, and national researchers to work jointly on public health challenges.
