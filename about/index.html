Our Identity

A Student-Led Society for Positive Social Change

The Noakhali Science and Technology University Anti-Narcotics Society (NSTUANS) is a student-led organization committed to building a drug-free, aware, and socially responsible university community.

The society aims to raise awareness about the harmful effects of narcotics and substance abuse while encouraging students to make informed and responsible choices.

Through awareness campaigns, educational seminars, workshops, research initiatives, and community engagement, NSTUANS seeks to promote a healthy lifestyle and strengthen the role of young people in preventing drug abuse.

Our Core Philosophy

We believe that a drug-free society begins with awareness, education, responsibility, and collective action. Our mission goes beyond the university campus, aspiring to contribute to a safer, healthier, and more conscious society.

Official Motto: "Say No to Drugs, Say Yes to Life."

How NSTUANS Makes an Impact

Pillar 1
