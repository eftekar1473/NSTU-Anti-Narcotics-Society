The Beginning of Our Movement

The Noakhali Science and Technology University Anti-Narcotics Society (NSTUANS) was established with a vision of promoting a drug-free campus and building a socially responsible generation.

The society was founded through the initiative of visionary students along with a group of enthusiastic and socially conscious scholars of Noakhali Science and Technology University. Their shared commitment to raising awareness about narcotics and preventing substance abuse laid the foundation for this organization.

Since its establishment, NSTUANS has been working to create awareness, encourage positive social values, and inspire students to take an active role in preventing drug abuse.
