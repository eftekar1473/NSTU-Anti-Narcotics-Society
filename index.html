About NSTUANS

Committed to a Drug-Free University Campus

The Noakhali Science and Technology University Anti-Narcotics Society (NSTUANS) is a student-led organization committed to building a drug-free, aware, and socially responsible university community.

The society aims to raise awareness about the harmful effects of narcotics and substance abuse while encouraging students to make informed and responsible choices. Through awareness campaigns, educational seminars, workshops, research initiatives, and community engagement, NSTUANS seeks to promote a healthy lifestyle and strengthen the role of young people in preventing drug abuse.

We believe that a drug-free society begins with awareness, education, responsibility, and collective action. Our mission goes beyond the university campus, aspiring to contribute to a safer, healthier, and more conscious society.

Key Organizational & Research Goals

NSTUANS operates under seven long-term organizational goals and seven evidence-based research objectives.

01

A Drug-Free Campus

Promote a safe, healthy, and drug-free environment within the university through active peer vigilance.

02

Raising Awareness

Educate students about the physical, psychological, social, and economic consequences of substance abuse.

03

Empowering Youth

Encourage students to become responsible leaders and proactive advocates of anti-narcotics initiatives.
