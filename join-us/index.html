Why Join the Anti-Narcotics Society?

As an NSTUANS member, you become an integral part of a campus-wide movement dedicated to student health, peer leadership, and creative awareness.

  • Engage directly in organizing awareness rallies, seminars, and workshops.
  • Collaborate with faculty and fellow scholars on empirical substance research.
  • Develop public speaking, event leadership, and peer advocacy skills.
  • Receive official certificates of social service and extracurricular distinction.
Membership Eligibility: Open to all currently enrolled students of Noakhali Science and Technology University (Undergraduate & Postgraduate). Applicants must pledge to uphold a drug-free life and support society principles.
