# NSTUANS Administrator User Guide

**Welcome to the NSTUANS Admin Portal!**  
This guide is written for university club executives, faculty advisors, and student organizers who manage the website. You do not need an IT or computer science background to use this guide. Everything is explained in simple, step-by-step everyday language.

**Society Motto:** *"Say No to Drugs, Say Yes to Life."*  

---

## 1. Accessing the Admin Portal

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari).
2. Go to: `https://nstuans.org/admin/login.html` (or `http://localhost:3000/admin/login.html` if running locally).
3. Enter your assigned **official email** and **password**.
4. Click **Sign In to Dashboard**.

> [!NOTE]
> Public visitors cannot register for admin accounts. Admin accounts are created strictly by the Super Admin to protect student information and campus society data.

---

## 2. Navigating Your Dashboard

After logging in, you will see the **Main Dashboard Overview**:
- **Live Statistics Cards:** Shows current counts of published events, photo albums, pending student applications, and unread messages.
- **Left Sidebar Menu:** Your navigation menu to move between different society management tools.
- **Sign Out Button:** Located at the top right and bottom of the sidebar. Always click **Sign Out** when you are finished using a shared university computer.

---

## 3. Managing Student Membership Applications

When students apply via the public **"Join Us"** page, their applications arrive securely in your admin portal. **Student data is private and never shown to the public.**

### Step-by-Step: Reviewing an Application
1. In the sidebar, click **Applications**.
2. You will see a table of student submissions. The badge at the top shows how many applications are waiting for review (**Pending**).
3. **Filter Applications:** Click the buttons at the top (**All**, **Pending**, **Approved**, or **Rejected**) to view specific groups.
4. **Search:** Type a student's name, Student ID, or department in the search bar.
5. **Open Details:** Click the gold **Review Application** button next to any applicant.
6. A review box opens showing:
   - Full student name and student ID.
   - Academic department and batch session.
   - Email address and mobile phone number.
   - The student's written statement on why they want to join NSTUANS.
7. **Add Private Notes:** Under *Internal Admin Notes*, you can type any comments (for example: *"Interviewed on campus 5th Nov, verified current enrollment"*). These notes are completely confidential.
8. **Take Action:**
   - Click **Approve Application** to welcome the student to NSTUANS.
   - Click **Reject** if the applicant does not meet enrollment criteria or fails verification.
9. You will see a green confirmation toast notification, and the status badge will update instantly.

---

## 4. Managing Contact Messages & Inquiries

When campus visitors, professors, or journalists submit inquiries on the **Contact** page, they arrive in your **Inbox Messages**.

### Step-by-Step: Handling Inquiries
1. In the sidebar, click **Inbox Messages**.
2. **View a Message:** Click **Read** on any message row.
   - The full inquiry opens on your screen.
   - The message is automatically marked as **Read**.
3. **Replying to the Sender:** Click the gold **Reply via Email** button. This automatically opens your email program (Outlook, Gmail, etc.) with the sender's email address and inquiry subject already filled in.
4. **Archiving:** Once an inquiry has been answered, click **Archive** to keep your inbox clean.
5. **Deleting:** If you receive spam, click **Delete** and confirm.

---

## 5. Publishing Society Events

You can post announcements for upcoming seminars, workshops, rallies, and scientific conferences.

### Creating a New Event
1. In the sidebar, click **Events**.
2. Click the gold **+ Create New Event** button at the top right.
3. Fill in the event details:
   - **Event Title:** e.g., *Campus Anti-Narcotics Awareness Rally 2026*
   - **Slug (URL shortcut):** e.g., `campus-rally-2026`
   - **Category:** Select *Seminar*, *Awareness Campaign*, *Workshop*, etc.
   - **Event Date & Time:** When the event will take place.
   - **Location:** e.g., *NSTU Central Auditorium*
   - **Cover Image URL:** Paste the web link to the event banner or photograph.
   - **Detailed Description:** Explain the event's purpose, key speakers, and how students can join.
   - **Publication Status:**
     - Choose **Draft** if you are still working on it and not ready for the public to see it.
     - Choose **Published** when you want it to appear immediately on the public website.
4. Click **Save Event**.

---

## 6. Managing Leaders and Advisors

The public **Leaders** page displays our respected Faculty Advisory Board and Student Executive Committee.

### Adding or Updating a Leader
1. In the sidebar, click **Leaders & Advisory**.
2. Click **+ Add Leader / Advisor**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Full Name:** Official academic name.
   - **Position:** e.g., *Chief Faculty Advisor*, *President*, *General Secretary*.
   - **Department / Affiliation:** e.g., *Department of Pharmacy, NSTU*.
   - **Role Category:** Choose *Advisory Board* or *Executive Committee*.
   - **Profile Photo:** Link to a clear professional photo.
   - **Verification Status:** If official university committee paperwork is still pending, mark as *Pending Verification*.
4. Click **Save**.

---

## 7. Managing the Photo Gallery

Keep the campus community inspired by uploading photographs of society activities.

### Creating an Album
1. In the sidebar, click **Photo Gallery**.
2. Click **+ Create New Album**.
3. Enter the album title (e.g., *Poster Presentation Competition 2026*), date, and a brief description.
4. Click **Create Album**.
5. You can now add photographs to this album. These photos will automatically be viewable by the public in the interactive fullscreen image viewer (lightbox).

---

## 8. Society Information & Site Settings

Super Administrators can edit the official society text displayed across the website:
1. In the sidebar, click **Settings & Content**.
2. You can review and update:
   - **Mission Statement:** The core purpose of NSTUANS.
   - **Strategic Vision:** Long-term goals for student wellness.
   - **Society Motto:** Set to *"Say No to Drugs, Say Yes to Life."*
   - **Authorized Admin Accounts:** View existing admin emails and assign roles.

---

## 9. Security Best Practices for Administrators

To protect student privacy and university data:
- **Never share your password:** Each administrator must have their own login account.
- **Always Sign Out:** Do not leave your admin dashboard open on shared or public university computers.
- **Verify Student Credentials:** Cross-check student ID numbers with university department records before approving memberships.
- **Adhere to Data Integrity:** Never publish unverified dates or names. If a historical fact is unverified, leave it marked as pending verification until confirmed by university administration.
