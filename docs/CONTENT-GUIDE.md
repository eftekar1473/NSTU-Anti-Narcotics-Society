# NSTUANS Content Management & Editorial Guide

**Project:** Noakhali Science and Technology University Anti-Narcotics Society (NSTUANS)  
**Target Audience:** Content Editors, Media Coordinators, Publications Committee  
**Society Motto:** *"Say No to Drugs, Say Yes to Life."*  

---

## 1. Editorial Voice & Communication Guidelines

As the official voice of the Noakhali Science and Technology University Anti-Narcotics Society, all written content, event descriptions, and media captions must adhere to the following principles:

1. **Constructive, Dignified & Empowering:** Focus on student well-being, scientific awareness, positive alternatives, peer mentorship, and healthy living. Avoid sensationalist, stigmatizing, or fear-mongering language.
2. **Academic & Research-Driven:** Emphasize empirical data, university workshops, behavioral health insights, and expert guest seminars.
3. **Strict Truthfulness & Data Integrity:**
   - **Never invent or assume** founding dates, past committee names, or historical statistics.
   - If an archival record or executive name is unverified, it must remain clearly designated as `Pending Official Verification` until officially confirmed by university gazette or society archive.
4. **Mandatory Motto Inclusion:** The official motto &mdash; *"Say No to Drugs, Say Yes to Life."* &mdash; must be prominently displayed across key publications, press releases, and major event announcements.

## 4. Step-by-Step Content Workflows

### 4.1 Publishing an Event
1. **Title:** Use an active, descriptive title (e.g. *"Annual Campus Anti-Narcotics Research Idea Contest 2026"*).
2. **Slug:** Use lowercase letters and hyphens only (e.g. `research-contest-2026`).
3. **Date & Time:** Always include time and expected duration (e.g. `10:00 AM - 1:00 PM BST`).
4. **Location:** Specify hall name and building (e.g. `Auditorium, Academic Building 1, NSTU`).
5. **Description Structure:**
   - Paragraph 1: Purpose of the event and keynote guests.
   - Paragraph 2: Agenda / scheduled activities (paper presentation, rally, interactive Q&A).
   - Paragraph 3: How students can register, participate, or volunteer.
6. **Status:** Set to `Draft` while reviewing internally; set to `Published` only after faculty advisor sign-off.

### 4.2 Adding a Photo Gallery Album
1. **Album Title:** Clearly indicate event name and year (e.g. *"NSTUANS Inaugural Rally & Banner Campaign"*).
2. **Event Date:** Enter the actual date the photographs were taken.
3. **Image Captions:** Provide brief, respectful descriptive captions for each photo (e.g. *"Faculty advisors and student leaders inaugurate the awareness procession across NSTU campus"*).
4. **Ordering:** Place the strongest establishing shot as the cover image.

### 4.3 Updating Leadership & Advisory Rosters
1. Ensure the individual's full academic designation and university department are spelled correctly.
2. If adding a student executive member, specify their committee term (e.g. `Executive Committee 2025-2026`).
3. If formal appointment documentation is pending, check the `Pending Official Verification` box so public visitors understand the roster is being finalized.
