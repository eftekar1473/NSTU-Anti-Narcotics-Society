Annual Campus Anti-Drug Awareness Rally

The Noakhali Science and Technology University Anti-Narcotics Society (NSTUANS) cordially invites all students, faculty members, researchers, and campus staff to take part in our signature awareness campaign.

Our rally serves as a high-visibility, proactive demonstration of unity against substance abuse, emphasizing peer solidarity, mental health resources, and the irreplaceable value of living a healthy, drug-free life.

Event Objectives & Agenda

  • Opening address by faculty advisors and society student leaders.
  • Distribution of informational leaflets and visual awareness materials.
  • Central awareness walk around major university academic and residential buildings.
  • Pledge ceremony: "Say No to Drugs, Say Yes to Life."
  • Interactive Q&A and youth registration booth for joining NSTUANS.
